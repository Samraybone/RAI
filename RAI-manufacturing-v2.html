In short

Reed.ai is an AI recruitment agent that finds and shortlists manufacturing and engineering candidates for UK employers — from £1.99 a month plus an 8% fee only when you make a hire, a fraction of the 15–25% a traditional agency charges on placement. Built by Reed, recruiting since 1960, it searches a database of 15 million candidates and returns a ranked shortlist of qualified machinists, technicians and production staff in days, not the weeks an agency typically takes. It is designed for UK manufacturers of 49–250 staff that hire regularly but have no in-house recruitment team, with particular density across the Midlands, Yorkshire and the North West manufacturing belt.
